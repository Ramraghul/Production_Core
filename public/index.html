    What this is

    A working Manufacturing Execution System for a vehicle assembly plant, built on Node-RED. Seven production lines and 43 stations — body shop, paint, door line, wheel & tire, sub-assembly, main assembly and end-of-line quality — with real domain rules behind them.

    What it actually enforces

    • Routing. A vehicle cannot skip a station. Attempts are rejected, not logged.
    • Quality gates. A unit carrying an open critical defect does not move, and cannot be released.
    • Genealogy. Every part is recorded with its supplier lot at the point of use. The record is append-only and sealed at release.
    • Single consumption. A serialised sub-assembly can be fitted to exactly one vehicle, ever.
    • VINs. Real ISO 3779 identifiers with valid check digits — they decode in any off-the-shelf VIN decoder.

    Where to look

    Node-RED flows → 12 tabs, 183 nodes, 7 custom nodes. Generated from a declarative plant spec. REST API (Swagger) → 71 operations. Try POST /trace/recall first. Line board → An HTML page served by the Node-RED flows themselves. Architecture notes → Why the domain is a library and the flows are the orchestration.

    Standards it follows

    ISA-95 / IEC 62264 plant hierarchy · ISO 22400-2 OEE and KPI definitions · ISO 3779 VIN structure · Six Big Losses downtime classification.
